# SAP ERP Customer Portal

A web-based Customer Portal integrated with SAP ERP that provides customers with a single platform to access their business and financial transactions.

## Overview

The portal allows customers to securely log in and view their SAP data through a clean and user-friendly web interface. It covers key Sales & Distribution (SD) and Finance (FI) processes.

## Features

- 🔐 Customer Login & Authentication
- 👤 Customer Profile
- 📋 Inquiry Management
- 🛒 Sales Order Tracking
- 🚚 Delivery Management & Tracking
- 💰 Invoice & Financial Details
- 📊 Payment & Aging Information
- 🧾 Credit/Debit Memos
- 📈 Transaction Dashboard & Charts
- 🔎 Search, Filtering & Sorting
- 📄 Pagination & Data Export
- 📦 Expandable Item-Level Details

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** Angular
- **UI:** Angular Material
- **Backend:** SAP ECC / S4
- **Integration:** SAP RFC Webservices
- **Programming:** ABAP
- **Database:** SAP Database

## SAP Integration

The application retrieves customer-specific business data from SAP through RFC Webservices and displays it in the Angular portal.

The portal covers the following transaction flow:

**Inquiry → Sales Order → Delivery → Invoice → Payment**

## Project Scope

The project focuses on implementing SAP **Sales & Distribution (SD)** and **Finance (FI)** business processes through a modern web-based customer portal.
